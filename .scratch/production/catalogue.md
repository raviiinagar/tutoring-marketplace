# Initial India learning catalogue

The owner confirmed all eight domain groups below on 10 October 2026. The product is an online tutoring contact marketplace; these groups organise tutor discovery and do not establish course delivery, mock-test functionality, tutor availability or proven market growth.

| Learning domain | Initial tracks and subjects |
|---|---|
| School education | CBSE, ICSE/ISC and state-board curricula; school subjects |
| Entrance examinations | JEE, NEET, CUET and other engineering/medical entrance preparation |
| Higher education | Engineering, medicine, commerce, science and humanities tutoring |
| Competitive examinations | UPSC, SSC, banking, railway and state examination preparation |
| Professional qualifications | CA, CS, CMA and other qualification preparation |
| Career skills | Programming, data science, AI, design and accounting |
| Languages and communication | English, Indian/foreign languages and interview preparation |
| Arts and other learning | Music, drawing and other tutor-led skills |

## Classification rules

- Learning domain groups related needs; it is not the subject itself.
- Subject describes what a tutor teaches, such as Physics, Biology or Mathematics.
- Exam track identifies the learner's target examination, such as JEE or NEET; it may span several subjects.
- Learner level describes school class, academic stage or skill proficiency.
- School board/curriculum applies where relevant; a career-skills listing does not need a school board.
- Teaching language is declared by the tutor; the initial application interface is English.
- Exam-specific profiles identify the target examination, subjects, learner level, teaching language and relevant tutor experience.
- A tutor may cover multiple relevant domains/tracks through accurate listings; exam and board labels alone are not evidence of qualification.

## Availability

Publish accurate available listings. A catalogue group with no available tutors displays its availability state and an optional notification signup. Paid requests are enabled only when an eligible tutor exists to receive them.

## Demand evidence

Use official participation/enrolment evidence to distinguish large demand from measured growth. Broad category support is an accepted product decision; promotion priorities and supply acquisition should follow validated demand and response coverage.
