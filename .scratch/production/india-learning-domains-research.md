# India learning domains: evidence and catalogue proposal

Research date: 10 October 2026 (IST). Primary official sources only; observation years are stated separately from publication dates. This research supports the owner's request for broad initial coverage including JEE and NEET. It proposes catalogue structure and examples; it does not promise tutor availability in every category. VEER is the current owner; the business/platform name is undecided.

## What the evidence supports

| Area | Latest verified official observation | Interpretation for the marketplace |
|---|---|---|
| School learning | UDISE+ reports **24.72 crore students in 2025–26**. [Official UDISE+ portal](https://udiseplus.gov.in/) | A large learner population supports school subjects across boards and classes. Enrolment is not a count of paying tutoring customers. |
| Engineering entrance | JEE Main Paper 1 recorded **16,04,854 unique registered candidates in 2026**, compared with **15,39,848 in 2025**: **4.22% growth**, calculated from comparable unique annual registrations. [NTA 2026 results, candidate table](https://www.nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20260420194021.pdf), [NTA 2025 results](https://www.nta.ac.in/Download/Notice/Notice_20250419125449.pdf) | Evidence for large, recently growing engineering-entrance participation. Count unique annual candidates, not the sum of January and April registrations. |
| Medical entrance | NEET UG recorded **22,79,743 registrations in 2026**, versus **22,76,069 in 2025** and **24,06,079 in 2024**. Registrations grew only **0.16%** in 2026 over 2025 and remain below 2024. The 2026 appearance count is **19,99,895**. [NTA historical data, page 1](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/07/202607161539405935.pdf) | Strong established scale; do not call it rapid current growth. The final exam was held on 21 June after re-examination, so appearance changes need that context. [NTA result release, 16 July 2026](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s37bc1ec1d9c3426357e69acd5bf320061/uploads/2026/07/20260716477215762.pdf) |
| University entrance | CUET UG recorded **15,68,867 unique registrations in 2026**, versus **13,54,699 in 2025**: **15.81% growth**, calculated. Appearances rose from **10,71,735** to **11,64,098**. The 2026 test offered 37 subjects, including languages, domain subjects and general aptitude. [NTA 2026 results, pages 1–2](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3d1a21da7bca4abff8b0b61b87597de73/uploads/2026/06/20260623716111016.pdf) | Evidence for broad university-entrance coverage beyond PCM/PCB. Registrations, subject-test instances and appearances are different measures. |
| Higher education | AISHE 2023–24 estimated **4,50,01,123 learners**, versus **4,46,32,261 in 2022–23**: **0.83% growth**, calculated. Regular-mode BCA enrolment rose from **7,12,491** to **8,65,410**, about **21.46%**. [AISHE 2023–24, Box 12 and Box 14, printed pages 55 and 58](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s392049debbe566ca5782a3045cf300a3c/uploads/2026/07/202607131602421770.pdf) | College tutoring and computing are plausible categories. Programme enrolment growth is not proof of paid tutoring demand; college subjects and curricula need their own filters. |
| Emerging technical learning | More than **2,100 AICTE-approved institutions** offered AI and other emerging-area courses in 2025–26, with **approved intake above 2.80 lakh**. [Ministry of Education, 3 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2294001&lang=1&reg=22) | Supports including programming, data/AI and technical skills as candidate categories. Approved intake is capacity, not actual enrolment or paid demand; no comparable annual growth rate is claimed. |
| Broader exam preparation | SATHEE covers JEE, NEET, CUET, ICAR, SSC and IBPS; more than **21 lakh learners** had registered by August 2026. SWAYAM Plus offered over **500 courses across 15 sectors** in July 2026. [Ministry of Education, 3 August 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2294003&lang=2&reg=48) | Supports researching academic, recruitment and employability categories. These aggregate free-service figures cannot establish growth or paying demand for any individual exam. |

The latest AISHE reports were released on **8 July 2026**, although they describe academic years 2022–23 and 2023–24. [Official release](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=291005)

**Conclusion:** broad initial catalogue capability is reasonable. There is measured recent growth in JEE Main registrations, CUET UG registrations, and selected higher-education programmes. There is no evidence here that every Indian learning domain is growing. These populations overlap and must not be added together as a unique market size. No private tutoring revenue, conversion, or market-share projection follows from these data.

## Model dimensions separately

| Dimension | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Learning Domain | Broad discovery family | School learning; entrance/exam preparation; career skills |
| Subject | What the tutor teaches | Physics; Mathematics; English; Python |
| Learner Level | Educational stage, grade or proficiency | Class 11; undergraduate year 1; adult beginner |
| Curriculum | Board, university programme or applicable framework | CBSE; ISC; a particular university B.Tech curriculum |
| Exam Track | Optional preparation target | JEE Main; JEE Advanced; NEET UG; CUET UG Mathematics |
| Teaching Language | Language used to explain lessons | Hindi; English; Tamil; multiple languages |
| Learning Goal | Desired outcome where useful | Board preparation; entrance preparation; conversational fluency; interview practice |

Use structured **subject + level + curriculum + optional exam-track combinations** on a Listing. Independent arrays create false matches: a tutor teaching class 10 CBSE Physics and JEE Mathematics must not be returned as a JEE Physics tutor merely because both labels occur somewhere on their profile. One Subject may appear in several offers; one tutor can support multiple offers without duplicating identity.

Keep the language being taught separate from Teaching Language. Keep an exam year or syllabus version distinct from an academic class. A repeater preparing for JEE/NEET is not necessarily enrolled in class 12.

This proposes vocabulary for the domain-modeling workflow: Learning Domain, Subject, Learner Level, Curriculum, Exam Track and Teaching Language. Broad coverage should retain ADR 0003's online-first India scope and ADR 0002's guardian-controlled child access.

## Production implications and evidence gaps

- Make categories and curricula manageable by staff, with stable identifiers and aliases; prevent free-text duplicates such as JEE/JEE Mains/JEE Main from splitting discovery.
- Tutor approval must apply to the teaching claims that underpin each Listing. Broad category support does not imply that every tutor can teach every listed level or track.
- For an empty category, show accurate availability and capture learner demand; do not present fabricated Listings or permit payment against unavailable eligible tutors.
- Compare local search demand, responsive approved tutors, Contact Request acceptance, Connection conversion and No-match Refunds by offer category after launch. National enrolment and examination participation are useful context; these marketplace measures determine actual category readiness.
- No independently verified comparable Indian growth series for private language, arts, hobby or professional tutoring was found in this bounded research. Treat those as catalogue candidates requiring supply and demand validation.
