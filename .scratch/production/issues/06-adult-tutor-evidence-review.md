# 06: Onboard an adult Tutor and review private evidence

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task
Blocked by: 02 — Manage roles, account recovery and sessions; 03 — Sign in to a scoped staff console with MFA

**What to build:** A Tutor creates a profile and submits identity and relevant experience/qualification evidence; trust staff review it and the Tutor sees precise outcomes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Only adult Tutors aged 18 or older can qualify for publication; profile validation and correction states work.
- [ ] Uploads enforce ownership, allowlisted formats, size limits and scanning; unscanned/rejected files are inaccessible as approved evidence.
- [ ] Private evidence cannot appear in public responses; reviewer downloads are scoped, expiring and audited.
- [ ] Decisions specify the check, actor, outcome and expiry/revocation where relevant rather than a generic teaching-quality guarantee.
- [ ] Tests exercise cross-account files, malicious uploads, reviewer scopes and revoked verification.
