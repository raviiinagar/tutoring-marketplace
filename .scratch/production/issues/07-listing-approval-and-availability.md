# 07: Publish approved teaching offers and manage availability

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task
Blocked by: 05 — Browse and administer the eight-domain catalogue; 06 — Onboard an adult Tutor and review private evidence

**What to build:** A reviewed Tutor creates structured teaching offers, receives staff approval and can pause or update their available Listings.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Offers bind valid Subject, Learner Level, Curriculum and optional Exam Track combinations with rates, units, languages and trial terms.
- [ ] Draft, review, changes-requested, published, paused and safety-suspended states are enforced through API and UI.
- [ ] Sensitive claim/evidence changes return to review; a Tutor cannot remove a staff safety suspension.
- [ ] Public eligibility requires approved claims, active account and available Listing.
- [ ] Tests cover ownership, invalid combinations, edit/review transitions and staff-restricted resumption.
