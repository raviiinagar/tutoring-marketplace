# Short Tutor onboarding, public introductions and Tutor Pro

Owner decisions: name, Subject expertise, rate, about/skills/experience and one private identity ID; Indian government ID or foreign passport; public profile after identity/adult approval even without an offer/pass; profile photo/qualification evidence optional. Existing Lovable UI/components/design remain. Tutor Pro INR 600/30 days unlocks teaching offers and **consented public contact email**. Existing Learner INR 599 pass stays for in-app Connections. Reference discussion distinguishes this custom rule from Superprof's actual free teaching ads/optional Pro/private contacts.

Adopted defaults: hourly introductory rate in INR, up to ten active Subjects; 18+ self-confirmation plus manual staff identity/adult review (no mandatory duplicate DOB entry in the short form); skills/experience explicitly self-declared; no contacts in public name/about. One active identity submission, clean malware scan, encrypted private file and scoped audited Trust review, expiry and revocation. Public email is the already-verified account email and requires explicit versioned Tutor consent explaining anonymous visibility. No phone is published without a separate verified-phone workflow. India permits Aadhaar/other government photo ID/passport; other countries require passport. Do not collect an identity number as a form field. Optional portrait remains separately reviewed, never extracted from ID.

Separate immutable Learner/Tutor Pro commercial purposes, product-scoped coupons and purchase locks. Tutor Pro activation starts at server-confirmed capture (or full coupon redemption), fixed 30 days with no auto-renewal. Confirmed provider facts use existing capture/refund/reconciliation journal. No learner matching/no-match rule applies to Tutor Pro. Public contacts require current identity, Pro, an eligible published offer and consent; no cache of expired rights. Existing safe private Connections survive ordinary Pro expiry. Product-specific live disclosure approval remains required; test mode and zero-price coupon are available without live launch assumptions.

Legacy evidence/history survives; legacy approved profiles are backfilled only from actual Subjects/rates, never invented. New public-profile search is distinct from exact teaching-offer search; advanced level/exam matching still uses actual offers. Deletion removes public projection/consent and revokes Pro rights. Ordinary profile edits retain current reviewed identity unless identity fields change or the Tutor explicitly replaces the document.

Enquiries validly submitted while Tutor Pro is current retain their recorded response deadline after ordinary Pro expiry; identity, safety, blocks, offer revision and Learner coverage are still rechecked at acceptance. Pre-rollout valid pending enquiries receive the same protection. New requests and public contact still require current Pro.
