# Tutoring Marketplace

A marketplace connecting learners with tutors. The platform's contact service and the tutor's teaching service are distinct offerings.

## Language

**Learning Domain**:
A broad area of learning, such as school academics, entrance-exam preparation, higher education or professional skills.
_Avoid_: Subject, exam when referring to the broad grouping.

**Subject**:
The specific area of knowledge or skill a tutor teaches, such as Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or programming.
_Avoid_: School board, exam track.

**Exam Track**:
A learner's target examination or examination family, such as JEE or NEET, which can involve several subjects.
_Avoid_: Subject when referring to an examination goal.

**School Board**:
The education board governing a school learner's curriculum, such as CBSE, CISCE or a state board.
_Avoid_: Exam track, subject.

**Curriculum**:
The educational framework or programme a teaching offer follows, such as a school-board syllabus or a university programme.
_Avoid_: Subject, exam track when referring to the governing educational framework.

**Teaching Language**:
The language used to explain lessons, which may differ from a language being taught as the Subject.
_Avoid_: Subject language when referring to the language of instruction.

**Learner Level**:
The academic stage or skill proficiency for which tutoring is sought, such as a school class, undergraduate study or beginner proficiency.
_Avoid_: School board, exam track.

**Learner**:
The person receiving tutoring, who may be an adult or a child represented by a guardian.
_Avoid_: Customer when referring specifically to the lesson beneficiary.

**Guardian**:
The adult responsible for a child learner's platform access, payments and communication.
_Avoid_: Child account owner when referring to the child.

**Tutor**:
The person offering teaching services through a marketplace listing.
_Avoid_: Platform employee unless an employment relationship is explicitly established.

**Listing**:
A tutor's public offer describing the subjects, learner levels, teaching mode, rates and terms offered.
_Avoid_: Lesson, booking.

**Contact Request**:
A learner or guardian's enquiry asking a particular tutor to establish contact about tutoring.
_Avoid_: Confirmed lesson, booking.

**Connection**:
An authorised relationship through which a learner or guardian and an accepting tutor can communicate and access permitted contact details.
_Avoid_: Completed lesson, attendance.

**Connection-linked Review**:
Feedback from a connected learner or guardian who attests to actual interaction with the tutor.
_Avoid_: Verified lesson review, proof of attendance unless lesson attendance is independently established.

**Access Pass**:
The platform product granting time-limited rights to establish new tutor connections.
_Avoid_: Tuition fee, lesson payment.

**Pass Activation**:
The beginning of an Access Pass's usable period once its payment and first eligible tutor acceptance qualify.
_Avoid_: Checkout completion, tutor acceptance alone.

**No-match Refund**:
A return of the platform access charge when a purchase receives no eligible tutor acceptance by its matching deadline.
_Avoid_: Tuition refund, completed bank credit when only refund initiation is known.

**Refund Initiation**:
The submission of an approved return of money to the payment provider, preceding its confirmed outcome and any bank processing.
_Avoid_: Refund received, money returned when the outcome is still pending.

**Tuition Fee**:
The amount a learner or guardian pays a tutor for teaching, separately from the platform's access charge.
_Avoid_: Platform revenue, access fee.
