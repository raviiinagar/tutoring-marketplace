# Use guardian-controlled access for child learners

> **Superseded by ADR0015 (9 November 2026).** Child profiles and Guardian accounts are retired; a parent books from their own adult account.

Children are served through guardian-controlled accounts, with the guardian responsible for payment and communication, rather than independent child accounts. This preserves support for school-age learners while placing account and contact authority with an adult; guardian authority checks, consent and staff handling still need detailed decisions before release. The user confirmed this boundary on 10 October 2026.
