# Publish identity-reviewed profiles separately from paid teaching offers

On 28 October 2026 the owner replaced qualification-heavy onboarding with a short Tutor introduction and one private identity document: Indian government ID (including Aadhaar) or passport for a non-Indian Tutor. Identity/adult approval permits a public profile with declared Subjects, rate and self-described skills/experience; it does not verify teaching expertise. Photo/qualification uploads are optional. In the new flow identity changes require a new review, while ordinary public introduction edits retain the approved identity.

The owner explicitly selected a separate **Tutor Pro Pass, INR 600 for 30 days**, unlocking creation/publication of teaching offers and, with the Tutor's explicit consent, public contact email while an eligible offer and Pro access are current. This intentionally differs from Superprof's optional promotion product and private contact model. The existing INR 599 Learner Access Pass remains for in-platform Connections; public contacts can be used outside that learner paywall. Products, coupons, payment facts and access grants remain distinct. Renewals remain explicit one-time actions; no automatic recurring debit is introduced.

Existing money history and established safe Connections are preserved. Public identity documents, exact birth dates and private evidence never enter the public projection. Revoked/expired identity, account safety restrictions or unavailable current publication remove public eligibility; Pro expiry/refund removes new-offer/public-contact rights while the identity-approved profile remains visible. Live Tutor Pro checkout needs its own approved commercial disclosures; ordinary staging can test through a separate zero-price Pro coupon without fabricating payment.

Enquiries validly submitted while Tutor Pro is current retain their recorded response deadline after ordinary Pro expiry; identity, safety, blocks, offer revision and Learner coverage are still rechecked at acceptance. Pre-rollout valid pending enquiries receive the same protection. New requests and public contact still require current Pro.
