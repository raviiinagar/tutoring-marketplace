# Publish teaching ads directly and separate identity verification

On 4 November 2026 the owner explicitly requested Superprof-style direct ad publication and confirmed: **free ads; identity verification optional**. This supersedes ADR0011 and the production specification's mandatory identity/claim review and Tutor Pro gates for public introductions, ad publication and receiving new enquiries. A verified email, an adult declaration, complete valid teaching details, catalogue/location rules and current account safety still apply. The Learner Access Pass and established Connection authorization remain unchanged.

Profile publication and identity-review status are separate facts. Explicit owner publication can make a complete introduction/ad public without a reviewer; identity remains unverified until an actual current identity review succeeds. Drafts stay private until publication. Live edits take effect immediately after validated save; an ad can be paused/resumed or archived without deleting established history. Identity documents remain private and optional. Clean consented portraits can publish after scanning without fabricated staff approval. Existing approved identity/photo records and all financial history are retained.

Existing adverse staff decisions and safety restrictions are not silently cleared or bulk-published. Staff keep audited restriction/lift tools; a lift does not automatically publish a private draft. No client-only status change can bypass server restrictions. Existing online supply is preserved, while old draft/rejected/revoked cases are not automatically exposed by migration.

Tutor Pro becomes optional for ads. Existing grants, purchases and refunds are preserved without retroactive charges, automatic renewals or invented promotion/ranking benefits. The earlier explicit public-email-consent and current Pro rule remains separate from free ads; ordinary learner enquiries keep their existing access/payment rules. Verification and higher reputation levels must never be inferred merely from publication or a pass purchase.
