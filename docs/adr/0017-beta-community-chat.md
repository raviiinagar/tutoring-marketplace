# Beta community chat

On 10 November 2026 the owner asked for a live community where beta members can talk with each other and the team. This follows ADR0015, which requires any open discussion feature to admit only eligible adults, and the owner's decisions recorded in ADR0016.

**Who takes part.** Signed-in accounts that are not restricted can join. Sign-up has no age check (ADR0015 kept the 18+ confirmation on profiles), so joining asks for an explicit 18-or-older confirmation and acceptance of the community rules, both recorded with the rules version. Members pick a nickname that other members see instead of their account name. Nicknames that look like a team account, or that contain contact details, are refused. Signed-out visitors see nothing. There are no private messages and members cannot create channels. Members can change their nickname or leave, and leaving ends their access straight away, including any live stream.

**Channels.** The migration seeds Announcements (only the team can post), General, Bug reports, Feature ideas, and Help & support. Help & support points account and payment problems to Support. Posting in a team-only channel needs a verified staff session (`TRUST` or `SUPPORT` scope with MFA). Team posts show a Team badge, which is worked out from the author's current staff access; it is never something a member can claim.

**Messages.** Text only, 1–1,000 characters, with replies to a message in the same channel and six fixed reactions. Phone numbers, emails, social handles and links are refused, the same rule that enquiries follow, so the community cannot be used to bypass paid contact access. Authors can delete their own messages. Posting is limited to 8 messages a minute and 300 a day, reactions to 120 an hour, and reports to 20 a day. Retrying a post with the same request key returns the original message.

**Real time.** Delivery reuses the existing chat pattern: a server-sent event stream woken by PostgreSQL `NOTIFY`, which re-checks the session, membership and channel on every frame. Every visible change takes a new per-message change number, so clients catch up with a single cursor. Writers lock the channel row so change numbers are recorded in the order they are issued, and a client can never skip a slower concurrent write. If a proxy buffers the stream, the client falls back to polling the same endpoint. No new websocket service or infrastructure is added.

**Moderation.** Any member can report another member's message. Staff with the `TRUST` scope and MFA review open reports and see the message, its channel, the author's account id and the report notes. They can remove (and restore) a message, pin or unpin one, dismiss reports, mute a member for up to a year or lift a mute, and create, rename, restrict or archive channels. They cannot mute themselves. Every view and action records a reason in the audit log. A muted member can still read but cannot post or react.

**Privacy and the switch.** Message text and report notes are encrypted at rest. Privacy export includes the membership, the account's own messages, reactions, reports filed and any mute. Account deletion removes the membership, reactions and mute, redacts the account's messages to deleted and redacts its report notes. Other members then see "Former member". The feature sits behind `BETA_COMMUNITY_ENABLED`, which is on by default outside production and off in production unless it is set to `true`. Migration 040 is additive.

**Deferred.** Message editing, attachments, mentions, unread counts, notifications and search are not included. A minor-safe community remains out of scope until it is designed separately.
