# Assisted Tutor onboarding

On 14 November 2026 the owner asked for a second way to become a Tutor: instead of filling in the wizard, a Tutor can send their details and documents to the team, and a team member sets up and publishes the profile for them. The owner decided that the team does everything, including publishing; that the Tutor sends details and documents by WhatsApp (team number 8233598649) or email; and that every admin with Support access can do this work. This amends ADR0014, under which only the owner publishes their own profile and ads.

**The Tutor's choice and consent.** After signing up, a Tutor can either set up their profile themselves or ask the team. Asking the team records a request with the Tutor's own consent, versioned `assisted-onboarding-v1`: that they are 18 or older, that the team may create, edit and publish their profile and ads using what they send, and that their photo may be shown publicly. The Tutor also gives the WhatsApp number they will message from, and the request carries a short reference (for example `TM-4F7K2A`) to quote in that message. One request can be active at a time. The Tutor can withdraw it at any time, which ends staff access immediately; a later request needs fresh consent.

**What staff can do.** Staff with the `SUPPORT` scope and MFA see the queue, with the Tutor's account name, email, WhatsApp number and note. A team member claims a request before working on it; another can take it over or release it. While a request is claimed, staff act for the Tutor through the same services and rules as the Tutor's own wizard: introduction, location and teaching modes, photo upload, ads, and publishing or pausing. Every existing check still applies, including photo scanning, catalogue and location rules, publication requirements and safety restrictions. Staff cannot change the Tutor's consent to show their email publicly, cannot set up their own profile, and cannot act on an open, completed or cancelled request. Completing or cancelling closes the request; cancelling needs a reason.

**Records.** Every staff view and action writes an audit event naming the staff member, the request, the Tutor and the action. The Tutor's own introduction and publication events record who assisted. Assisted publication is still owner-authorized publication (`direct-ads-v1`): it never sets identity verification or adds a badge, which still need a real identity review.

**Documents outside the platform.** WhatsApp and email are the owner's chosen channels for receiving documents. The staff page reminds team members to upload what is needed into the platform and then delete the documents from WhatsApp and email, so that privacy export and account deletion cover them. The platform cannot reach those copies.

**Privacy.** The WhatsApp number and note are encrypted. Privacy export includes the request, its consent and the contact details. Account deletion cancels an active request and erases the contact details and staff note.

**Not included.** No email or in-app notification is sent when the team completes a request; the Tutor sees the status on their Teaching page, and the team is in touch over WhatsApp. Identity documents are not collected through this flow; optional identity verification stays separate.
