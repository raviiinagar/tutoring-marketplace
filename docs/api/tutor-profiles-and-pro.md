# Tutor introductions and Tutor Pro

POST /v1/me/tutor/introduction saves the short form (name, about, active subject_ids, hourly_rate_paise, identity_country IN/OTHER, identity_document AADHAAR/GOVERNMENT_ID/PASSPORT, adult_confirmed true, public_email_consent, replace_identity, expected_public_revision). Existing profiles require their public revision. Other-country identity requires PASSPORT. An explicit save may add the ordinary TUTOR role; it never grants staff access.

POST /v1/me/tutor/identity accepts one PDF/PNG/JPEG up to 1 MiB, scans privately, then atomically attaches the identity claim and submits the current identity revision. No qualification, duplicate DOB, claim-description or portrait step is mandatory. Changing identity name/country/type or choosing replacement starts a new identity revision. Subject/rate/about/consent edits retain reviewed identity. Trust MFA and current private-document inspection/adult attestation are still required.

GET /v1/tutor-profiles supports subject_id and cursor, at most 24 introductions. GET /v1/tutor-profiles/:publicId returns the free identity-approved introduction, self-declared expertise labels and current eligible offers. It never returns internal account IDs, birth dates or document metadata. Approved clean portraits have an independently reauthorized photo URL. Email is included only with versioned explicit consent plus current Tutor Pro and an eligible published offer. No phone is published. Full account erasure clears publication metadata/consent and revokes Pro; exports include the new metadata and grants.

GET /v1/me/tutor/pro returns the INR600/30-day policy, active interval and saved Pro purchase. POST /v1/me/tutor/pro/purchases accepts request_key, policy_version tutor-pro-v1, optional coupon_code. The identity-approved Tutor owns the purchase. Retry keys, open purchases, unknown provider attempts, server prices, journals and fixed activations are authoritative. Main live paid Tutor Pro checkout remains unavailable pending distinct approved commercial disclosures; Razorpay Test is supported in the isolated sandbox. A 100% Pro coupon creates an immediate fixed 30-day non-cash Pro grant without an Order, capture or Learner entitlement. No automatic renewal and no Learner 72-hour/no-match refund rule apply to Pro.

Coupon campaign creation and quote accept product LEARNER_ACCESS (default) or TUTOR_PRO. Codes cannot cross products. Learner current-purchase/coverage queries exclude Pro. Combined payment history and receipts label each product. Paid Pro cancellations before activation and reviewed refunds use the existing financial workflow; revocation removes new-acquisition/public-contact rights. Valid submitted requests keep their response deadline across ordinary Pro expiry, with current identity/safety/offer revision/Learner coverage checks. Established safe Connections remain available.
