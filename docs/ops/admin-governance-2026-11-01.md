# Super Admin and delegated review access - 1 November 2026

The owner requested one Super Admin, delegated review/support Admins, and no authenticator prompts during the current staging testing phase. [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-single-super-admin-and-bounded-test-authentication.md) records the bounded exception to ordinary mandatory staff MFA. [API and operator configuration](../api/admin-governance.md) describe the boundaries and endpoints.

The existing verified main-site owner is now the sole configured Super Admin. All other staff accounts in a governed deployment are limited to effective TRUST/SUPPORT permissions. Only the Super Admin can grant/revoke delegated access to existing active verified accounts; the protected owner cannot be revoked or reassigned through the UI. Revocation and re-grants invalidate old staff sessions. Staff access remains separate from ordinary account roles, and review-purpose, participant/self-review and safety checks remain intact.

The Lovable staff workspace now has a Super Admin-only Manage admins page with email, required reason, explicit revoke confirmation, enabled/revoked status and a protected owner row. Ordinary Admins receive the referenced Tutor evidence/profile/experience, offers, portraits, Guardian, safety, review moderation, support/appeals and privacy tools. They cannot access finance/coupons, audience/account dashboards, analytics, operations, notification administration, catalogue administration or delegation through direct APIs. Navigation and direct-page handling reflect these permissions. Login refreshes the shared permission cache immediately.
